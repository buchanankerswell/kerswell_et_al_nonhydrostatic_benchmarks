## Findings and formulation revisions (`notes/feedback-formulation.md`)

Taking the divergence of the compressible Stokes momentum equation with shear viscosity $\eta$ and bulk viscosity $\zeta$ yields $\nabla^2(\hat{P} - M\,\nabla\cdot\vec{u}) = \nabla\cdot(\hat{\rho}\,\vec{g})$ with longitudinal viscosity $M = \frac{4}{3}\eta + \zeta$, so $\hat{P} = \hat{P}_\mathrm{buoy} + M\,\nabla\cdot\vec{u}$. In the continuum limit, combining this relation with projected-density continuity gives a stable first-order ODE with decay rate $\lambda_\mathrm{cont} = (1 + M\gamma\kappa)/(M\bar{\beta}) > 0$. What oscillates is the time-discretized operator-split `single Advection, single Stokes` loop: `compute_reactions()` and the prescribed `density_field` interpolation run before `assemble_and_solve_stokes()`, so $\rho_\mathrm{field}^n = \rho(T^n, P^{n-1}, X^n)$ uses the lagged Stokes pressure $P^{n-1}$. Finite-differencing $\rho_\mathrm{field}$ in `StokesProjectedDensityFieldTerm` gives the backward-Euler recursion $p^n = -(a+b)\,p^{n-1} + a\,p^{n-2}$ (step 1, unstable when $2a + b_\mathrm{eff} > 1$) and the BDF2 recursion $p^n = -\frac{3}{2}(a+b)\,p^{n-1} + \frac{1}{2}(4a+b)\,p^{n-2} - \frac{1}{2}a\,p^{n-3}$ (steps $n > 1$, unstable when $2a + b_\mathrm{eff} > 1/2$), where $a = M\bar{\beta}/\Delta t$, $b = M\gamma\kappa$, and cell-averaging across a reaction front of thickness $\delta w \approx 3.5\;\mathrm{km}$ gives $b_\mathrm{eff} \approx \min(1, \delta w / \Delta y)\,M\gamma\kappa$. At the 410 in the sink suite ($Z_\mathrm{ol} = 4.44\times 10^{-4}\;\mathrm{K^{-1}\,s^{-1}}$), $a \approx 0.036$ and $b \approx 2.58\,(1 - X)$.

The $\sim 100\;\mathrm{MPa}$ relaxed-pressure offset is resolved. In `point_values.txt` at probe 0 ($(500\;\mathrm{km}, 635.8\;\mathrm{km})$), the true settled Stokes pressure $P$ at $t = 5.0\;\mathrm{Ma}$ is $13\,756.5\;\mathrm{MPa}$ for `DynpOff` (`S2`), $13\,757.1\;\mathrm{MPa}$ for `DampNone` (`S1`), and $13\,757.0\;\mathrm{MPa}$ for `Rel3.156e11` (`S7`, with $P_\mathrm{rel} = 13\,754.2\;\mathrm{MPa}$) — agreeing within $0.6\;\mathrm{MPa}$. The $-100.7\;\mathrm{MPa}$ offset in reported `nonadiabatic_pressure` (`p - adiabatic_pressure`), as well as the $-20.41\;\mathrm{MPa}$ offset in `DampNone` and $-5.1\text{--}13.0\;\mathrm{MPa}$ offsets in `Cap`, arose because `NHKinetics::evaluate()` applied `dynamic_pressure` during `AdiabaticConditions::ComputeProfile::initialize()` when `!this->get_adiabatic_conditions().is_initialized()`. In `Rel` runs, `evaluate()` read `p_drive` from the unrelaxed $3900\;\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$ initial composition `2.0e9 + 3900*g*(1e6-y)` (`initial.prm:8`), compressing the reference density by $\sim 39\;\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$ and baking $+100.7\;\mathrm{MPa}$ permanently into `adiabatic_pressure` at the 410 depth (and $+220\;\mathrm{MPa}$ at the bottom of the domain, which also inflated `final_range_MPa` from $\sim 178\;\mathrm{MPa}$ to $\sim 395\;\mathrm{MPa}$).
