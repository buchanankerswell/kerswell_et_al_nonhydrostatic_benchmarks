## Framing

The manuscript asks three questions: how nonadiabatic pressure affects the 410/520/660 phase transformations and seismic structure; how large the effect is; and how to implement the feedback stably in compressible ASPECT. Compressible ASPECT applies instantaneous density changes in the continuity equation while kinetic rates are driven by dynamic pressure; with zero bulk viscosity the loop oscillates (Gassmoller et al. 2020). S1 is the undamped full-feedback baseline, S2 the no-feedback reference. The 25-run sink suite varies damping, mesh (Res5-7) and CFL; Z is fixed at Z_MID for now.
