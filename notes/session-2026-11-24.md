  `relaxed-mean-check.csv` and `relaxed-mean-check-sink.png`. It flags the
  ~100 MPa settled-mean offset in all seven relaxed runs.

  and `rel_err_vs_reference` (fixed S2) for every run, including mesh and CFL
  variants whose paired controls never completed.

  when a resolution has no no-feedback twin, so `displacement-error-sink.png`
  keeps its Res5 and Res7 points.
